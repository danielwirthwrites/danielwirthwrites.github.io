Author site palettes

Every scheme here is a committed dark identity — one fixed set of colours, no light-mode switch. The first three below turned out too safe, so Round two takes the brief literally: race-kit, custom-paint, hazard-sticker loud. In all of them the loud colour lives on headlines, links and buttons; body paragraphs stay near-white so pages of prose and poetry are still readable.

Recommendation — Round two

05 — Flip Paint is the pick for “out of this world”: a chameleon magenta→violet→cyan→lime shift, the same trick as colour-flip motorcycle paint, run across the masthead and buttons only. The ground stays near-black and the reading text stays calm, so it's wild without being unusable.

04 — Two-Stroke if you want it loud but simpler: gunmetal, fluoro orange, electric cyan. 06 — Toxic for maximum aggression: matte black, radioactive lime, hazard yellow.

Round two — loud

04

Two-Stroke

Gunmetal-black with a fluoro-orange primary and an electric-cyan second accent, plus an acid-lime spark for small details. Straight off a race graphics kit.

05

Flip Paint

Near-black with a colour-shift “chameleon” gradient — magenta, violet, cyan, lime — that drifts across the masthead, the rule and the button. Links use a solid hot magenta. The gradient animates slowly; it holds still for anyone with reduced-motion set.
