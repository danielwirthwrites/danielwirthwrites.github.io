Preview — palette 06 “Toxic”. The colour-shift flows continuously in one direction and loops seamlessly. The cycle runs lime → yellow → a deep burnt orange (#d95e00) and back over ~28 stops, with the green-to-yellow stretch broken into many close steps so it eases all the way through to orange with no visible step, and the burnt orange sits on a soft plateau rather than a point. One single gradient is pinned to the viewport, so the masthead, the top bar, the divider and the buttons are all windows onto the same sheet — identical angle, speed and phase throughout. One full loop takes 22 s. Links get a gradient wipe on hover. All motion freezes for reduced-motion settings. Body text stays flat bone-white so prose and poems read cleanly.
