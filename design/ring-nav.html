Move the cursor left or right to turn the wheel

Fiction & poetry.

Novels and poems, and the occasional note from the desk.

The five sections sit on a wheel, “About” at the top to start. Move the mouse right of centre and the wheel turns left; left of centre and it turns right — further out spins faster; near either edge it stops. Move the cursor onto or inside the ring and it stops there and highlights the nearest section, ready to click. Drop the cursor below the ring and it also stops (easing the nearest section under the marker). Click or tab to a section to bring it to the top. Motion is disabled if your system is set to reduce motion.
