About

German by origin, New Mexican by altitude. Writes poems sitting on the ground.

  • Origin Rheine, a small town in Germany. Born in the 1980s.
  • Habitat The mountains of New Mexico, in a stretch of forest that doesn’t ask questions.
  • By day Research technologist.
  • By night Forest dweller and writer — in that order, according to the dogs.
  • Fieldwork Walks the woods with two dogs, looking for oddities. Comes back with fewer than he’d like and, now and then, a poem.
  • The reason Poetry did the job that drugs and alcohol were never invited to do. He has been broken in half twice — both times literally: two broken spines, no wheelchair either round. He stays faintly annoyed at the odds.
  • Wakes at 4:47. The military set the alarm and never came back for it.
  • Writes on the ground. Any ground. A chair has yet to improve a line.
  • Keeps a slowly expanding collection of plush keychains. No theory has survived contact with the evidence.
  • Cooks like it’s a dare. The more steps, the better.
  • Method Bad at most things; does them anyway. That is the entire method.
  • Reads poetry, science fiction, eco-fantasy, nonfiction. He once read The Library at Mount Char halfway through a manuscript, decided he hated his own style, and rewrote the book to be more mysterious. That book is now in the toilet, where he left it.
