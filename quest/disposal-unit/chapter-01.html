CH. 1 — WAKE CYCLE

[ PLACEHOLDER CHAPTER — sample text standing in until the real Chapter 1 is sent over. ]

The pod told Reyes she’d been under for six years before it told her anything useful, like where they were, or why the count on the wall read five sleepers instead of six.

Frost still clung to the inside of the glass. She watched it melt in patches shaped like nothing, and tried to remember whose bunk was closest to hers. The lights came up amber, which meant low power, which meant the ship had been making its own decisions for a while.

Somewhere aft, something that was not the recycler cycled on.
